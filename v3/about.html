Our Story & Sourcing Philosophy

Crafting honest, slow-fermented bread for our local community since day one.

Unrushed Baking, Simple Ingredients

At North Star Bakery, we believe that exceptional bread cannot be rushed. Every morning before dawn, our bakers mix regional whole grains with pure water, sea salt, and our wild sourdough starter—nurtured continuously for over seven years.

We work directly with local stone mills to source unbleached, non-GMO flour that retains its natural wheat germ, bran, and essential minerals. By allowing our doughs to cold-ferment for up to 36 hours, we achieve complex flavor development and a loaf that is gentle on digestion.
