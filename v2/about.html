Our Baking Story & Philosophy

Simple ingredients, local stone-milled grains, and patient fermentation.

The Art of Unhurried Baking

North Star Bakery was founded on a simple conviction: real sourdough cannot be rushed. In a world of commercial additives and fast yeast, we honor time-tested baking traditions.

Every loaf begins before sunrise with wild yeast starter cultures, regional stone-ground flour, pure filtered water, and sea salt. Our doughs cold-ferment for up to 36 hours, unlocking deep complex flavors and breaking down gluten for easier digestion.

Local Sourcing Philosophy

We source 100% of our flour from independent regional mills that work directly with sustainable wheat farmers. By supporting organic crop rotation, we ensure our bread tastes exceptional while caring for local soil health.
